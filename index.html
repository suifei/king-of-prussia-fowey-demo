Town Quay, Fowey

The King of Prussia

A historic pub with rooms, standing on Fowey's Town Quay for centuries. Restored — old-world charm with a touch of modern comfort.

Six bedrooms look over the Fowey Estuary.

The pub

Teresa and her team

Teresa and her team. Head chef Scott Williams. Proper British food — comforting pub classics done right, and seasonal dishes.

A passionate kitchen, imaginative menus, local produce and suppliers. A well-stocked bar. A warm welcome to locals and visitors, at the heart of the town.

On the quay

Carter's

Carter's bar at The King of Prussia, named after smuggler John Carter. A homage to Cornwall's rebellious spirit and maritime history.

A quayside bar.

Before you come

Children, dogs, parking

Children

Children are welcome. Access is tricky, given the age and structure of the building. We are unable to accommodate pushchairs. There is a separate children's menu; we will adapt dishes for allergies.

Dogs

The restaurant is small — tell us at booking. Dogs should be dry and clean, not after a muddy walk or a swim.

Allergies

Dietary requirements: please tell us when you book. We will adapt dishes where we can.

Parking

Parking is limited. Use the public car parks around Fowey. You can drop off outside, then park.
